ExcelTab

In-Browser Spreadsheet & PDF Viewer Extension

Developed by Beacon IT Team
Overview

ExcelTab is a high-speed, privacy-first browser extension that intercepts spreadsheet and document download links (.xlsx, .xls, .csv, .ods, .tsv, .pdf) and opens them directly in an interactive browser viewer tab without writing files to your hard drive.

Key Capabilities
⚡
Zero-Download Flow Instant inline browser preview
🔒
100% Local & Private Processed inside browser memory
🎨
Style Preservation Retains colors, fonts & grid styling
🔍
Interactive Grid Search, zoom, formula bar & stats
